# 🚀 Welcome to Rat.io – Reddit Activity Tracker

**Rat.io** is an all-in-one team workspace platform designed for managing, tracking, and scaling Reddit marketing accounts, brand mentions, and organic community engagement.

---

## 🎯 Primary Purpose & Key Benefits

1. **Centralized Reddit Account Portfolio**: Track all team Reddit accounts in one clean spreadsheet view divided into **Group A (High Authority)** and **Group B (Secondary/Support)** accounts.
2. **Dynamic Account Health Score (0–100)**: Automatically calculates real-time account health based on activity recency, karma gains, post contributions, and comment frequency.
3. **Manager Approval Workflow**: Team operators submit Q&A comments and posts for manager review (`Approval Needed` ➔ `Approved` / `Changes Needed`).
4. **Minimal Performance Trends**: Visual trend indicators (`↑ +550` Green, `↓ -20` Red, `-` Blue) tracking Karma and Contribution growth without clutter.
5. **Team Workspaces ("Spaces")**: Secure space isolation using **Space ID** & **Space Password** so multiple team members and clients can collaborate safely.

---

## ⚡ Key Modules Overview

### 1. Executive Dashboard
- **Top KPI Cards**: Overall accounts, Active vs. Banned counts, Pending Manager Approvals, Brand Mentions (Posts/Comments), and General Engagements.
- **Side-by-Side Line Tables**: Group A accounts on the left, Group B accounts on the right for instant strategy comparisons.

### 2. Daily Activity & Master Accounts Table
- Manage full account details: Username, Reddit URL link, Persona, Country, City, Account Age, Status (`ACTIVE`, `WARMING`, `BANNED`), Karma Overall (Post/Comment), and Contribution Overall.
- Inline **Edit | Delete** actions for quick updates.

### 3. Comment & Post Tracker
- Log and approve Reddit outreach activities with explicit fields:
  - **Subreddit**: e.g., `r/SaaS`, `r/webdev`, `r/startups`
  - **Content Type**: `Brand Post`, `Brand Comment`, `General Post`, `General Comment`
  - **Q&A Content & Brand Mention**: Full text and target website links.
  - **Manager Remarks**: Real-time feedback and approval status notifications.

### 4. Interactive Google Calendar & Task Reminders
- Visual calendar overview of daily scheduled outreach activities.
- Day-before reminder alerts for pending posts and manager approvals.

### 5. Customization & Data Export
- 1-click **Light Mode ☀️ / Dark Mode 🌙** toggle.
- 1-click **CSV Data Export** for weekly client reporting.

---

## 👥 Quick 3-Step Guide for Team Operators

1. **Access Space**: Open the live app URL, click **Space Access** (top right), select **Join Existing Space**, and enter your **Space ID** and **Password**.
2. **Log Activities**: Go to **Comment & Post Tracker** -> Click **Submit Task** -> Enter subreddit, content type (`Brand Post`, `Brand Comment`, etc.), and Q&A content.
3. **Check Approvals & Health**: Monitor account health scores and manager approval feedback on the Executive Dashboard.
